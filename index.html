Is this a free airdrop claim?

No. This form sends a contribution to the organizer now. The organizer plans to distribute $LAPTOP tokens manually after launch. Participation does not set a fixed token price or guarantee an allocation.

How many tokens will I receive?

The organizer will decide the amount after launch. There is no fixed exchange rate between your contribution and the number of tokens, and no allocation is guaranteed.

Where does my contribution go?

Directly to the organizer's wallet shown in the form: 0x4704…991af4. You choose ETH or USDC on the Base network. Your wallet will ask you to confirm before sending.

What do I need in my wallet?

ETH or USDC on Base, plus enough ETH on Base to cover the network fee. Open this page in your wallet's browser or use a compatible browser extension. Use a wallet you control so the organizer can identify your sending address for the later distribution.

Can I change the amount or cancel?

You can change the amount before the payment request, or reject the request in your wallet. Once a transfer is confirmed, this site cannot undo it. Contribute only funds you can afford to lose.

When are the tokens sent?

The token has not been created yet. Distribution is planned after launch and will be handled manually by the organizer. An exact distribution time and token amount have not been set.
